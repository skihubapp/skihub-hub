# Written business enquiries

SkiHub welcomes a focused written enquiry about a potential service partnership, sponsorship, or investment discussion. This is an invitation to request information, not a representation that an agreement, investment round, operating metric, or commercial offer exists.

SkiHub is the main brand. KayakHocam.com and KiralıkKayak.com are positioned as customer and provider entry points. Read the [product overview](product.md) and [current evidence limits](status-and-roadmap.md) before relying on a feature claim.

## What to include

| Enquiry | Helpful initial information |
| --- | --- |
| Service or distribution partnership | Your organization, relevant location/service, proposed contribution, and the outcome you want to explore |
| Sponsorship | The intended audience, proposed scope and period, and what information you need to assess fit |
| Investment information | Your organization or role, written information requested, and the decision process or timing you can share |

Send your enquiry to [info@skihub.com.tr](mailto:info@skihub.com.tr). Written communication is the default; a call is optional and arranged only by agreement. Do not include sensitive personal documents, customer lists, credentials, or confidential deal terms in the initial message. Access to additional information is decided separately.

Sponsorship and paid visibility cannot bypass instructor verification, resort eligibility, availability, or customer-safety rules. No commercial discussion creates product-source access or administrative rights.

For technical participation, use [contribution paths](contribution-paths.md). Voluntary contributions do not carry a promise of pay, equity, employment, partnership, or commercial consideration.
