# Code of conduct

Treat people with respect. Discuss evidence and proposals rather than attacking a person. Use clear language, explain disagreement constructively, and welcome questions from people with different technical backgrounds.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, impersonation, unwanted contact, and sharing another person's private information are unacceptable. Do not pressure a contributor to disclose personal information, join a call, work for free, or accept an implied commercial commitment. Written communication is the default.

Keep issues and PRs relevant to their scope. A maintainer may redirect a discussion, remove harmful material, close a thread, or limit participation proportionately. Give a clear reason when it is safe to do so; protect reporters' privacy and avoid retaliation.

Report a concern privately to [info@skihub.com.tr](mailto:info@skihub.com.tr), with the subject **SkiHub conduct concern**. Include a brief description and safe references; do not repost abusive private content publicly. If the concern involves the initial reviewer, request a different authorized reviewer. Independent escalation contact: **to be assigned**. A conflict must be addressed before the person concerned adjudicates their own case.

This policy applies to participation in this repository and related contribution discussions. Participation does not create a promise of employment, pay, equity, or partnership. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for acceptance and rights boundaries.
